# Chlum V7 visual contract

The approved reference direction is a cinematic 2.5D South Bohemian field after rain.

## Camera
- fixed orthographic/isometric feel;
- smooth player follow;
- no player-controlled rotation;
- dead zone + damping;
- never expose space outside the authored world.

## Environment
- one coherent authored terrain plate;
- wet dark soil, irregular furrows, puddles and tyre traces;
- South Bohemian hedges, fences, meadow vegetation and village silhouette;
- village and church belong to distant background depth;
- no visible tile repetition.

## Runtime layers
1. terrain plate
2. static props
3. actors and moving objects
4. foreground occlusion
5. FX
6. HUD

## Approval gate
Nesměň, Besednice and Slávie must not receive independent visual redesigns until Chlum is visually approved on desktop, iPhone portrait and iPhone landscape.
